Uncompromising Food Safety Standards

Rigorous Protocols from Receiving to Dining Table

At FoodBaskets Corporation, food safety is not an afterthought—it is the foundational pillar of our operation. Our dedicated Quality Assurance team conducts regular internal audits, microbiological assessments, and process inspections.

The FBC Safety Guarantee

Every food handler undergoes certified hygiene training, regular health screenings, and adheres to strict HACCP guidelines.

Strict FIFO Inventory System

We mandate strict First In, First Out (FIFO) stock rotation across all dry, chilled, and frozen inventories, ensuring peak ingredient freshness and zero waste.

Cold Chain & Temperature Controls

Continuous digital data logging for refrigeration units and delivery vehicles. Food is held strictly within safe thermal zones during prep, transport, and holding.

Dedicated QA Internal Audits

Our independent Quality Assurance department executes unannounced inspections, swab tests on prep surfaces, and adherence audits across all concession sites.

Sanitation & Hygiene Protocols

Mandatory hand-washing checkpoints, hairnets, sanitary gloves, color-coded cutting boards, and medical-grade food contact surface sanitation chemicals.
